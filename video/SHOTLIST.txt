1. Close everything except Terminal. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
2. Terminal → Settings → Text → font size 18+ (viewers must read it).
3. Fresh receipt for a clean money shot:  ./timecop-cli clean
4. Rehearse each shot once without recording.
