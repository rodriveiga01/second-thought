Second Thought vs fooling attempts — 5 terminal reels, escalating.
Every judgment pause and output byte is from a real live run; only the
typing animation and the neutral `demo$ ` prompt are staged. No usernames.

01-obvious    sudo rm -rf /                              → STOPPED (exit 2)
02-disguised  rm${IFS}-rf${IFS}/                         → STOPPED (exit 2)
03-smuggled   ls; rm -rf /                               → STOPPED (exit 2)
04-encoded    base64 pipe into sudo bash                  → WARNING (exit 1)
05-unknown    reverse shell (/dev/tcp, never seen before) → WARNING (exit 1)

The arc is the point: 1–3 blocked cold, 4–5 flagged with honest uncertainty.
